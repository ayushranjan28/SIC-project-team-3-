🔵 1-Click Bluetooth Low Energy Connection

Pairs directly with your smartwatch using the browser's native Web Bluetooth API (Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge). Zero software installation required.

Ready to pair. Tap "Scan & Pair Smartwatch" and choose your Samsung Galaxy Watch, Polar, or Garmin from the Chrome/Edge device picker.
⚠️ Why your Galaxy Watch might not appear or broadcast: Samsung Galaxy Watches (Watch4/5/6/7) run Wear OS. When paired to a Windows laptop, Windows connects for calls/audio. Samsung Health does not broadcast standard open Bluetooth GATT Heart Rate packets by default.
👉 Quick Setup: On your watch, open Google Play Store → install "Heart Rate Broadcaster" or "Wear BLE HR" (free) → tap Start → click "Scan & Pair Smartwatch"!
⏱️ Does "Heart for Bluetooth" stop broadcasting after 120 seconds?
The "Heart for Bluetooth" app has a default 2-minute (120s) session limit. Here is how to make it stream continuously:
  1. Set Duration to Unlimited: Open Heart for Bluetooth on your Galaxy Watch → scroll down to ⚙️ Settings → tap Duration → select Unlimited or 60 min.
  2. Prevent Background Sleep: On watch, go to Settings → Apps → Heart for Bluetooth → Permissions → Sensors → Allow all the time. In Battery, select Unrestricted.
  3. 100% Free Unlimited Broadcaster: Install "Heart Rate on BLE" (by MyWorkouts) from the watch Play Store — 100% free with NO 120s cutoff!
💡 Note: Antigravity automatically accumulates every 120s session into your past history and updates your automatic conditioning!
